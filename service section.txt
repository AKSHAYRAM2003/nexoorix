## Why this works

These five services naturally group your capabilities:

| Service                       | Covers                                                |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Custom Software**           | Web apps, SaaS, portals, APIs, product engineering    |
| **AI Automation**             | Workflow automation, integrations, business processes |
| **AI Agents & Generative AI** | AI agents, chatbots, RAG, LLM-powered applications    |
| **Cloud & Data**              | DevOps, cloud, security, analytics, infrastructure    |
| **Digital Growth**            | SEO, AI SEO, performance, conversion optimization     |

This structure is easier for visitors to understand and still represents everything Nexoorix offers.

### Suggested scroll order

1. **Build** → Custom Software
2. **Automate** → AI Automation
3. **Intelligence** → AI Agents & GenAI
4. **Scale** → Cloud & Data
5. **Grow** → Digital Growth

That sequence tells a complete story:

**We build your product → automate your operations → add AI intelligence → scale your platform → grow your business.**

It's concise, memorable, and positions Nexoorix as a partner that delivers end-to-end digital transformation rather than a collection of unrelated services.
